GOAL

SUMMARY RULES

Use each task’s effective due date after considering:

- lastDone
- intervalDays
- Smart Start baseline
- skip overrides
- safety status
- local calendar date

Do not calculate summaries from raw seed dates.

Do not count tasks completed today as overdue or due today.

Use non-overlapping categories:

- Overdue
- Due today
- Later this week: 1–7 calendar days from today
- Unconfirmed safety checks
- Completed today

TODAY VIEW

Keep the existing calm format, but include overdue tasks when applicable.

Examples:

- “2 overdue · 5 due today”
- “5 due today”
- “You’re caught up today”

A smaller secondary line may say:

- “12 coming up this week”

If an unconfirmed safety task exists, show it separately:

- “1 safety check needs confirmation”

Do not combine later-this-week tasks into a statement that implies they must be completed today.

THIS WEEK VIEW

Use a concise format such as:

- “2 overdue · 5 today · 12 later this week”
- “5 today · 12 later this week”
- “You’re caught up for the week”

Omit categories whose count is zero.

Keep unconfirmed safety checks separate.

ALL TASKS VIEW

Use a neutral catalog count:

- “126 tasks”

Do not imply all catalog tasks are due.

A smaller secondary summary may show:

- “5 due today · 12 later this week”

FILTER CONTEXT

Verify how the current summary behaves with room and task-search filters.

Desired behavior:

- With no room or search filter, summarize the whole home.
- When a room is selected, summarize that room.
- When task search is active, summarize the matching results.
- The global “X of Y done today” progress indicator must remain based on the complete catalog and must not shrink with filters.

Examples:

- “Kitchen is caught up today”
- “2 due today in Master Bathroom”
- “No matching tasks due today”

Do not announce changing summary counts to VoiceOver after every search keystroke. Meaningful changes after Done, Skip, room selection, and view selection may use the existing restrained polite live region.
